# Data dictionary

All data are **synthetic** (generated by `backend/app/data/synthetic.py`). IDs are synthetic (`SYN-CUST-0000001`). No personal identifiers exist.
Economic indicators are synthetic proxies, **not official statistics**. Storage: one Parquet file per table in `data/synthetic/<dataset>/` plus `manifest.json` (config, row counts, SHA-256 fingerprint).

| Table | Key fields | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| `repayments` | repayment_id, loan_id, scheduled_due_date, scheduled_amount, paid_amount, actual_payment_date, days_past_due, repayment_status, recorded_at | Six instalments *before* time zero per loan. |
| `interventions` | intervention_id, customer_id, loan_id, intervention_type, assignment_date, delivery_date, intervention_cost, assignment_status, delivery_status, eligibility_at_assignment, assignment_probability, agent_or_channel_group, cohort_month | One assignment record per decision, including `none`. ## Model features (pre-treatment)

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| financial_condition | log_monthly_income, expense_ratio, debt_to_income_ratio, installment_to_income, financial_stress_indicator |
| repayment_history | historical_delinquency_count, historical_on_time_payment_rate, credit_history_length, account_tenure_months, prior_intervention_count |

Outcome columns, assignment, cost, delivery, and ground truth are excluded (test `test_no_outcome_or_treatment_features`).

## Eligibility rules (observed fields at time zero)

* standard_reminder: contactable
* counselling: contactable and (≥1 prior delinquency or stress indicator > 0.5 or DTI > 0.40)
* repayment_plan: not a credit line, ≥6 months remaining, and (DTI ≥ 0.35 or ≥2 prior delinquencies)

These are simulated business rules, not legal or regulatory criteria.

## Synthetic data-generating process

Latent traits: financial stress *s*, forgetfulness *f*, hidden income shock *u*. Baseline logit
`-1.55 + 0.85 s + 0.50 f + 0.35 econ + 0.60 u − 0.25 tenure_z + loan-type shift + 0.10 prior_delinq`.
Effects on the logit scale (each customer's potential outcomes share one uniform draw `U`: `Y(a) = 1[U < p_a(x)]`):

* **reminder**: strongly helps forgetful, low-stress customers (`−1.1·σ(1.8f − s − 0.2) + 0.12`), slightly harms the rest;
* **counselling**: helps stressed customers, attenuated by economic stress;
* **repayment plan**: helps high-DTI, high-stress customers a lot and *burdens* low-stress customers (+0.55·σ(−2.5 s)).

Delivery probabilities 0.92 / 0.70 / 0.85; delivered customers get `Y(a)`, others `Y(none)`, so the ground-truth ITT effect is `delivery × (p_a − p_none)`.

Scenarios: **randomized** (known propensities, fixed arm weights among eligible arms), **observational** (assignment is a softmax of *observed* risk: confounded but adjustable), **observational_hidden** (additionally depends on `u`, which is never exposed: unadjustable by construction, detectable by the placebo check).
